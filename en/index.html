How it works

Four steps. Zero renaming.

You decide the order, Numora assigns the numbers. Your hands stay on keyboard or controller – no mouse needed.

  1. 01

    Load photos

    Drag & drop, pick files or use “Open folder”. Numora sorts naturally by file name and shows everything in a grid of large thumbnails – size adjustable with a slider.

  2. 02

    Navigate & tap +

    Use WASD or the arrow keys to move through the grid, + assigns the next number. − undoes the last step – as often as you like.

  3. 03

    Check & swap

    Mark two photos with Enter, swap their numbers with X. K opens the selection window showing your series in order.

  4. 04

    Export

    Copies go into the subfolder “nummeriert” – or download everything as a ZIP. Your originals stay exactly as they are.

002
Features

Everything for the perfect series.

Wedding story, concert selects, photo book or social media series: Numora takes care of order and file names so you can focus on the pictures.

A Numbering & ordering

Photo by photo, in ascending order.

You assign the numbers in exactly the order you have in mind.

  • Load your way: drag & drop, file picker or “Open folder” – with natural sorting by file name.
  • Large thumbnails in the grid, size adjustable with a slider.
  • W A S D or arrow keys to navigate, + assigns the next number.
  • − undoes – a number or a swap, as often as you like.
  • A/B swap: mark two photos with Enter, swap their numbers with X.
  • No auto-jump: after numbering, the selection stays put – jumping ahead can be switched on if you like.
  • Live preview of the new file name under every photo.
DSC_4810.JPG → Wedding_001.jpg
B Windows

A large view that follows you.

Space bar or double-click: a freely movable window, resizable from every edge and corner, maximisable – and it follows your selection live.

C Windows

Selection window with K

Shows only the numbered photos in their order – perfect control over the series. One click jumps to the photo; zoom with −/+, a slider or Ctrl + mouse wheel.

D Options

Start number, prefix, digits.

Start at any number, add a prefix like “Wedding_” and choose 1, 2, 3 or 4 digits – or let Numora decide automatically.

E Automatic

Four digits from photo 1000.

With more than 999 photos, every file automatically gets four digits – so any software sorts your series correctly.

  1. 0001.jpg
  2. ⋮
  3. 0999.jpg
  4. 1000.jpg
  5. 1001.jpg

This also applies if you set the digits yourself: Numora always uses at least as many digits as needed.

F Two monitors

Detach & move.

The large view and the selection window can be detached as separate windows – for example onto a second monitor.

G Export

Folder or ZIP.

“Save to folder” creates copies in the subfolder “nummeriert”; alternatively “Download as ZIP”. Unnumbered photos can be exported too if you like.

  • Settings, design and key bindings are remembered
  • Asks before closing if you haven’t exported yet
  • Built-in help with H – always showing your current bindings

Controls

Keyboard or gamepad. Your choice.

Numora can be used entirely with the keyboard and supports game controllers such as Xbox and PlayStation pads. Holding a direction repeats the movement.

Keyboard

  • WASDNavigate (or arrow keys)
  • +Assign next number
  • −Undo last step
  • EnterMark photo (A/B)
  • XSwap numbers of marked photos
  • EscClear marks / close window
  • SpaceLarge view
  • KSelection window
  • TSwitch design
  • HHelp with current bindings

Game-Controller

  • ✚ / LSNavigate (hold = repeat)
  • AAssign number
  • BUndo
  • XMark
  • YSwap
  • RBLarge view
  • RTSelection window
  • LBClear marks
  • ViewSwitch design
  • MenuHelp
Fully customisable

Your keys, your rules.

Two keyboard keys and two controller buttons per action. Just click, press a key, done. Numora resolves duplicate bindings automatically – and remembers everything.

FAQ

Good questions.

Q01How much does Numora cost?

Nothing. Numora is free – no subscription, no account.

Q02Does Numora work offline?

Yes, completely. There’s no cloud and no account, and no photos leave your PC.

Q03Are my originals changed?

No. With “Save to folder”, Numora creates copies in the subfolder “nummeriert”. Alternatively you can download everything as a ZIP. Your originals stay unchanged.

Q04Which controllers are supported?

Game controllers such as Xbox and PlayStation pads and others. Default layout: D-pad/stick navigate, A = number, B = undo, X = mark, Y = swap, RB = large view, RT = selection window, LB = clear marks, View = switch design, Menu = help.

You can rebind every action – with up to two controller buttons and two keyboard keys.

Q05What happens with more than 999 photos?

From photo 1000, Numora automatically switches to four digits (0001 … 1000) so any software sorts the order correctly. Alternatively you set the number of digits yourself: 1, 2, 3 or 4.

Q06Is Numora available for Mac or Linux?

Currently Numora is only available for Windows (10/11, 64-bit).

Q07Why does my antivirus warn me?

Numora is not (yet) digitally signed. Some antivirus programs (e.g. Avast) or Windows SmartScreen therefore warn on the first download – a known false alarm for new, unsigned programs. In SmartScreen, click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

To verify it, you’ll find the installer’s SHA-256 checksum in the download section.

Q08How do I uninstall Numora?

Through Windows as usual, like any other program.

Q09Do I need administrator rights?

No. Installation works without administrator rights and creates Start menu and desktop shortcuts.

Q10How can I support Numora or give feedback?

The easiest way is the Support & feedbacksection: there you can buy me a coffee via PayPal or send me an email. You can also report bugs on GitHub.
